Can we use your camera?

This study can measure where you look on the screen while you choose. It is optional — the tasks work exactly the same either way.

  • The video never leaves your computer. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Only an estimate of where on the page you were looking is saved.
  • There is a short setup step first: click nine dots.

Can you see yourself?

Your camera preview is in the top-left corner. Sit so your whole face fits inside the box, at a comfortable distance from the screen.

From here on, try to keep your head still — that is what keeps the estimate accurate.

Look at each dot and click it Three clicks per dot. Keep your head still and try to look right at the dot as you click.

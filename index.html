Can we use your camera?

This study can estimate roughly where you look on the screen while you choose. It is optional — the tasks work exactly the same either way.

  • The video never leaves your computer. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Only an estimate of where on the page you were looking is saved.
  • There is a setup step first: click nine dots, five times each. It takes about a minute.

Stop eye tracking?

Your camera turns off and nothing more is recorded. What was already measured is kept. The tasks carry on exactly as before.

This cannot be turned back on for this run.

Can you see yourself?

Your camera preview is in the top-left corner. Sit so your whole face fits inside the box, at a comfortable distance from the screen.

Good, even light on your face helps a lot. Avoid a bright window behind you.

From here on, keep your head as still as you can — head movement after calibration is the main reason the estimate drifts.

Look at each dot and click it Five clicks per dot, nine dots. Look straight at each dot as you click it, and keep your head still from here until the end — moving is what makes the estimate drift.
